Checklist of documents before an inspection of a foreigner's employment

Published: 26 July 2026 · Author: Dariusz Włodarczyk Kancelaria TRC · Reading time: approx. 5 min

Good documentation is the best protection against sanctions. We gather in one checklist everything that needs to be checked before allowing someone to start work and after hiring, together with the deadlines to keep an eye on.

Table of contents
  1. Before allowing work to start
  2. After hiring
  3. Documentation for the inspection
  4. Deadlines to keep an eye on
  5. Legal basis and sources

1. Before allowing work to start

  • A valid residence document and a basis for work (permit/declaration/exemption),
  • the position and salary matching the document,
  • a written contract in a language the employee understands.

2. After hiring

  • Submitting the contract to the authority through the system,
  • notification of taking up work (as a rule, 7 days),
  • ZUS registrations (ZUA/ZZA) and PIT settlements.

3. Documentation for an inspection

Keep in one place: the residence document, the basis for work, the contract, confirmations of submitting the contract and notifications, and ZUS registrations. This will make a PIP/Border Guard inspection easier.

4. Deadlines to watch

Watch the dates: validity of the residence document and of the permit, renewal deadlines and notification deadlines. See extending a permit.

A 15-minute mini audit

For each foreign worker check three things: (1) whether the stay is valid, (2) whether there is a basis for work consistent with the contract, (3) whether obligations towards the authority and ZUS have been fulfilled. A missing element in even one of these is a signal for urgent correction.
