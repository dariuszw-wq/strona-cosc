Basis

Who is covered and what insurance provides

ZUS allowances depend on whether and to which insurance you are registered — not on citizenship. If you work legally and are covered by sickness insurance, you have the same access to them as a Polish citizen.

Employment contract

Sickness insurance is compulsory — you are covered from the first day of employment.

Mandate contract

Sickness insurance is voluntary — you must opt in (registration by the employer). Without it there is no sickness or maternity allowance.

Be sure to check this: ask the employer for confirmation of registration for sickness insurance (ZUS ZUA). With a mandate contract this is the most common reason for a benefit refusal.
Illness

Sickness allowance

It is granted after the so-called qualifying period — that is, after a certain time of uninterrupted sickness insurance:

SituationQualifying period
Compulsory insurance (employment contract)30 days
Voluntary insurance (mandate contract)90 days

Previous periods of sickness insurance count towards the qualifying period if the break between them did not exceed 30 days. The sickness allowance is, as a rule, 80% of the assessment basis (there are exceptions, e.g. 100% during pregnancy or for an accident on the way to/from work).

Family and child

Maternity and care allowance

Maternity allowance

On being covered by sickness insurance and the birth/adoption of a child. No qualifying period — from the first day of insurance.

Care allowance

For personal care of a sick child or another family member. Also no qualifying period. Annual day limits apply.

Longer incapacity

Rehabilitation benefit, accident, pension

Rehabilitation benefit

After the sickness allowance is exhausted, when further treatment or rehabilitation offers a prospect of regaining the ability to work.

Accident allowance

When the incapacity results from an accident at work or an occupational disease — from accident insurance, with no qualifying period.

Incapacity pension

A long-term benefit depending on the insurance history and a ruling on inability to work.

Retirement pension

Depends on the insurance history; contribution periods in Poland count towards a future benefit.

Practice

Which documents are worth gathering

To prove your right to a benefit efficiently, prepare: the contract (employment or mandate), confirmation of insurance registration (ZUS ZUA), payslips/salary confirmations, the medical certificate (e-ZLA) and — for a mandate contract — proof of opting into voluntary sickness insurance.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is a foreigner on a mandate contract entitled to sickness allowance?
Yes, if they opted into voluntary sickness insurance and the 90-day qualifying period has elapsed.
Does maternity allowance have a qualifying period?
No. Maternity and care allowance are granted from the first day of being covered by sickness insurance.
Does changing employer interrupt the service period for sickness allowance?
Previous periods of sickness insurance count if the break did not exceed 30 days.
Do periods of work in Poland count towards a pension?
Yes, contribution periods in the Polish ZUS count towards a future retirement or incapacity pension.
Legal basis

Legal basis and sources

Legal acts (as of July 2026)

  • Act of 25 June 1999 on cash benefits from social insurance in the event of sickness and maternity (the benefits act).
  • Act of 13 October 1998 on the social insurance system.
  • Act of 30 October 2002 on social insurance for accidents at work and occupational diseases.
  • Act of 12 December 2013 on foreigners (legal residence and access to the labour market).
