Before your first conversation

Have questions?
That’s a good start.

Here are a few practical tips. We will discuss your situation individually.

More questions and answers ↗
How do I contact COSC?+

Go to the contact page and briefly describe your case. You can also call +48 539 999 549 or email office@cosc.org.pl.

What should I prepare for the first conversation?+

Prepare a brief description of your situation, the history of your case and your questions. If you have received an official letter, mention it when contacting us. We will agree individually on the documents needed.

Where can I find your fees?+

You will find our packages and their scope in the COSC price list. Before we start working together, we agree on the scope of assistance for your case.

Can I start remotely?+

Yes. You can first contact us by phone or email. You will find our details and contact options on the contact page.

My case is already in progress. Can I contact you?+

You can also contact us about an ongoing case. Describe its current stage and any letters received so we can determine the appropriate assistance.
