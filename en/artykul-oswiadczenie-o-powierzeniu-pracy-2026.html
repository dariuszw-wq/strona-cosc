Declaration on entrusting work to a foreigner 2026 — who, where, how much and how

Published: 24 August 2026 · Author: Dariusz Włodarczyk Kancelaria TRC · Reading time: approx. 7 min

The declaration is the fastest route to legal work for a foreigner, but only for citizens of selected countries. We explain who it covers after the 2025/2026 changes, where to file it and how much it costs.

1. What the declaration is

A declaration on entrusting work is a simplified route to legalising work through entry in a register at the district labour office — faster and cheaper than a permit.

2. For citizens of which countries

Since 1 December 2025, the declaration applies to citizens of Armenia, Belarus, Moldova and Ukraine.

Change: Georgia has been removed from the list. A Georgian citizen with a declaration registered before 1.12.2025 may work until it expires.

3. Where, how and for how much

You file electronically via praca.gov.pl. Fee from 1.12.2025: PLN 400 (previously PLN 100).

Options and risks

An employee from outside the list of countries — a declaration is not enough; a permit or a single permit is required. For longer employment, consider a single permit right away to avoid a gap in legality.
