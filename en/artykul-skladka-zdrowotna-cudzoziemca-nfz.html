A foreigner's health contribution — 9%, the NFZ and registration with ZUS

Published: 26 July 2026 · Author: Dariusz Włodarczyk Kancelaria TRC · Reading time: approx. 6 min

A foreigner working legally is covered by health insurance just like a Polish citizen. We explain the amount and basis of the contribution, how to register with ZUS and access to NFZ benefits.

1. Who is covered by health insurance

The obligation of health insurance does not depend on citizenship. A foreigner legally performing work in Poland is subject to it on the same terms as a Polish citizen (Article 66 of the Act on healthcare benefits financed from public funds).

2. Amount and basis of the contribution

The health contribution is 9% of the basis. For an employee, the basis is income reduced by the social contributions financed from their own funds. Registration is made using the ZUS ZUA form (or ZUS ZZA, where only the health contribution is due).

Access to the NFZ: paying the health contribution gives entitlement to benefits under the NFZ; family members are also registered for benefits under the general rules.

3. Posted workers and coordination

For workers posted from the EU/EEA/Switzerland, coordination rules apply — access to benefits is confirmed by the appropriate documents (e.g. EHIC/A1). The Polish health contribution may then not be due.

4. Common mistakes

Note: the wrong NFZ branch on the registration, failure to register family members or omitting the contribution where insurance titles overlap are typical mistakes. Verify the data with ZUS.

ZUS ZUA or ZZA

ZUS ZUA is used to register for full insurance (social + health), while ZUS ZZA is used where only the health contribution is due. The choice of form depends on the insurance title and any overlap of titles.
