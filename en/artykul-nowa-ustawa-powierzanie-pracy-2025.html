The new act on entrusting work to foreigners — what changed on 1 June 2025

Published: 27 July 2026 · Author: Dariusz Włodarczyk Kancelaria TRC · Reading time: approx. 8 min

A new act on the employment of foreigners has been in force since 1 June 2025. We explain what changed for employers and HR — from the abolition of the labour market test to higher penalties — and how to prepare.

1. What this act is and since when it applies

This is the Act of 20 March 2025 on the conditions for permitting the entrustment of work to foreigners in the territory of the Republic of Poland (Journal of Laws 2025 item 621), in force since 1 June 2025. It replaced the previous provisions of the Act of 20 April 2004 on the promotion of employment.

2. Key changes

AreaWhat changed
Labour market testAbolished
Form of the procedureFull digitalisation (praca.gov.pl)
Names of permitsFrom 1.01.2026 new designations instead of types A–E
Contract obligationSubmitting the contract to the authority and notifying the start of work
SanctionsFine of up to PLN 50,000, National Labour Inspectorate fines of up to PLN 10,000

3. Abolition of the labour market test

The labour market test (the starosta's opinion) has been abolished — a real simplification of the procedure. Districts may, however, regulate the number of foreigners employed in the local labour market.

In practice: a shorter route to a permit, but more obligations after hiring and higher penalties for irregularities.

4. New obligations and sanctions

New obligations have been added: submitting the contract and notifying the start of work (as a rule within 7 days), and penalties for the unlawful entrustment of work have risen to PLN 50,000.

What to do step by step (employer)

Set up an organisation account on praca.gov.pl, update your HR procedures (who files applications and notifications), check whether the employee qualifies for a declaration or needs a permit, and keep complete documentation in case of an inspection.
