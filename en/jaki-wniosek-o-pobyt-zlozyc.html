Which residence application do you have to file? Choose your situation.

Published: 19 August 2026 · Author: Dariusz Włodarczyk Kancelaria TRC · COSC own material

There are more than a dozen types of application and each one has different documents and a different fee. Click the one that fits you — you will get a step-by-step guide written in plain language.

cosc.org.pl › Which residence application to file
You can file the application yourself — but you do not have to do it alone The MOS system is free and you can complete it without anyone's help. A lawyer will not issue the decision instead of the authority, and no honest person will promise you its content. What a representative does give you are three things you cannot secure on your own: an application filed without formal defects, control of deadlines and service of documents — the authority is obliged to serve letters on the representative — and a reaction when the authority exceeds deadlines or stays silent. A foreigner who does not know Polish procedure is the weakest party in these proceedings, and the representative's role is to make sure their rights are respected.

Check who you entrust your case to. A representative operating in Poland is answerable for their work under Polish law and you can establish who they are, where they are based and under what number they are registered. Be cautious about people who present themselves as experts in Polish regulations but cannot give those details.

Start here — three questions

If you do not know which card to choose, answer them one by one.

  1. Why are you in Poland? You work — go to the „Residence and work” section. You study — „Studies, family, other reasons”. You have family here — the same section.
  2. How long have you lived in Poland? Less than 5 years — you file an application for temporary residence. More than 5 years legally — check permanent residence or EU long-term resident.
  3. Do you already have an employer in Poland? If so and you want to settle residence and work with one decision — your application is the temporary residence and work permit. It is the most frequent choice.
Online or on paper? Since 27 April 2026 applications for a temporary residence permit, a permanent residence permit and an EU long-term resident permit are filed exclusively online in the MOS portal. Four types of cases are still filed on paper — below they carry the yellow label Paper only. The application for the issue or replacement of the residence card itself is also filed on paper.

Each fee is topped up by 100 zł for the residence card itself These are two separate transfers: the stamp duty for the permit and 100 zł for issuing the residence card (50 zł for a child under 16, a pupil and a student). The amounts are the same throughout Poland — only the account numbers differ, and you take them from the website of the authority handling your case.
Still do not know which application is yours? Filing under the wrong procedure is one of the most frequent reasons for refusal. The authority will not change it into the right one for you — you lose the fee and several months.
