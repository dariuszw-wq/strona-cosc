CUKR residence card — 3 years of stable residence for Ukrainian citizens with UKR status

Last updated: 7 July 2026 · Author: TRC Law Firm team · Reading time: approx. 7 min · UA/RU version coming soon

1. What the CUKR card is

The CUKR residence card (annotated “Formerly holder of temporary protection”) is a document confirming the grant of a temporary residence permit under special rules for a period of 3 years. It is intended for Ukrainian citizens who benefit from temporary protection in Poland (UKR status) and wish to move to a more stable basis of residence. Applications may be filed from 4 May 2026, exclusively online via the MOS portal.

2. Who can apply

  • a Ukrainian citizen (and certain family members) holding active UKR status (PESEL UKR),
  • residing legally in Poland on the basis of temporary protection,
  • who meets the conditions set out in the special provisions — we will verify the detailed criteria (including the period of holding UKR status) individually, because they depend on your situation.
An advantage: people with UKR status already have a PESEL number, so setting up a trusted profile and logging in to MOS is easier than for other foreigners. See: trusted profile for a foreigner.

3. What the CUKR card gives you

  • 3 years of legal residence — without worrying about further extensions of temporary protection,
  • full access to the labour market without a work permit,
  • the possibility of running a business,
  • travel within the Schengen area for up to 90 days in any 180-day period,
  • a step towards permanent residence or EU long-term resident status in the future.

4. What you lose — think it over before applying

After you collect the card, UKR status is converted into CUKR status. This means losing some of the entitlements arising from temporary protection, including the right to free accommodation in collective accommodation centres. If you use such benefits, it is worth consulting the decision to apply.

The most important deadline: collecting the card. Once the application has been decided favourably, the card must be collected within the set deadline — missing it may result in the card being annulled. Watch for notifications in MOS and summonses from the authority; do not plan long trips while you are waiting.

5. How to apply — in brief

  1. Set up a trusted profile (you have a PESEL UKR — identity confirmation or online banking is enough).
  2. Log in to MOS (mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl) and select the application for a CUKR residence card.
  3. Fill in the form (in Polish only), add a photograph and the required attachments.
  4. Sign electronically, submit, download the confirmation of submission (UPO).
  5. Wait for the summons for biometrics, then for the decision and the summons to collect the card.

Full instructions for using the portal: MOS step by step.

6. CUKR or another permit? A short comparison

CUKR cardOrdinary temporary residence (e.g. work)
Period3 yearsup to 3 years, usually tied to an employer
Access to workfull, without a permitusually with a specific employer (a change requires formalities)
ConditionsUKR status + special conditionscontract, income, insurance, place of residence
Better for whompeople with UKR status seeking stability and freedom on the labour marketpeople without UKR status or planning paths that CUKR does not cover

The choice of the optimal path depends on your plans (permanent residence, citizenship, family) — it is a strategic decision, not just a formality.
